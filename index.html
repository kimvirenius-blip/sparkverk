SparkVerk

SparkVerk is a relaxing idle clicker game for iPhone and iPad. Tap the glowing orb, buy generators, ascend and transcend, and catch golden sparks.

Support

Questions, problems or feedback? Contact me at duddens@hotmail.se. I usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

Where is my progress stored? On your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

How do I restore a purchase? Open the Shop tab and tap "Restore purchases".

How do I reset my progress? Open the Help tab and tap "Reset save".

Refunds for purchases are handled by Apple. Contact Apple through the App Store support pages.
