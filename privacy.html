Privacy Policy – SparkVerk

Last updated: 7 October 2026

SparkVerk ("the app") is developed by Kim Virenius, ASKOE 2, 725 91, Sweden ("I", "me"). Contact: duddens@hotmail.se.

Summary

The app does not collect your personal data on any server of mine. I have no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Your game progress is stored on your own device.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (energy, upgrades, settings such as sound, music and theme, achievements and quest progress) is stored locally on your device. I cannot see it. If you delete the app, this data is deleted. You can also delete all progress inside the app (Help tab, "Reset save").

Game Center (optional)

If you turn on Game Center in the app, the app uses Apple's Game Center to unlock achievements and submit scores to leaderboards. This data (for example your Game Center player name, achievements and scores) is processed by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. I receive no personal data from Game Center. You can turn Game Center off in the app and in your device settings.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. I do not receive or store your payment details, name or Apple ID. Apple tells me only aggregated sales information. The app checks with Apple which purchases you own so that they can be restored.

Notifications (optional)

If you allow notifications, the app schedules a local notification on your device (for example when your offline storage is full). It is created and delivered on your device only. You can turn it off in the app or in your device settings.

Third parties

The app does not use advertising, analytics or third-party SDKs. The only services involved are Apple's (App Store, StoreKit and Game Center).

Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed at children. I do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your rights

Because I do not collect or store personal data about you, there is normally nothing for me to access, correct or delete. Under the GDPR you still have the right to contact me with questions, and to complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY, www.imy.se).

Changes

If I change how the app handles data, I will update this policy and the date above.
